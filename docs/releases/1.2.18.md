# DC4I VOD TV 1.2.18 | Personal recommendations

Release date: 2026-10-25

## Highlights
- Per-profile "Dopasowane do profilu" Home recommendations with a transparent relevance score 0-100 and matching reason.
- Configurable genre preference, TMDB minimum rating for Sci-Fi (6/10), horror (9/10 or hide), other genres (7/10), and evening boost for family/romance.
- Local activity history with up to 150 opening events, scoped to each LG profile. Users may hide opened titles or clear their own activity history.
- Ratings originate from TMDB. An opening event is not represented as a completed watch.
- The existing webOS app ID `com.dc4i.vodtv`, all user Xtream settings and the family dedication are unchanged.
